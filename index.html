About this Explorer

What is this?

A static web app for exploring Artha Global's April 2026 study on scaling AI startups in India. It tracks both past government and ecosystem work already completed, and forward-looking recommendations prioritised by urgency and domestic control.

Content types

  • Past Efforts — government programmes, policies, and initiatives already launched (with links to sources).
  • Act Now — high domestic control, 0–3 year horizon. India has full policy agency.
  • Build Toward — structural reform needed, 3–7 year horizon.
  • Monitor — low domestic leverage; watch global trends.

How to update content

Open recommendations.json in any text editor or ask an LLM to edit it. Each entry has a content_type field (past_work or recommendation), a date_label, and a references array for external links. No build step needed.
